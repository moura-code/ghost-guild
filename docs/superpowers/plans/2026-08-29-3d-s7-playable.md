# Stage 7 — Make It Playable

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Executed inline, TDD where a test can see it, one commit per batch.

**Goal:** Close the gap between "the pivot is architecturally complete" and "a person can pick this up and play it." Menus, controls, readability.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-3d-pivot-design.md`. **Predecessor:** `2026-08-29-3d-s3-s6-the-game-in-3d.md`.

**Baseline:** 691 tests, 77 suites, green.

## The honest review

Taken from five screenshots of the **real** game — real `Crawl`, real HUD, real 3D — rendered by `tools/hud_shot.gd` in `docs/shots/review/`. Not from reading the code, because reading the code is what produced the problems.

### What is genuinely good

- **The environment holds up.** Materials, torchlight, fog, shadows and scale read as a commercial dungeon crawler. The stage 0 question is answered and stays answered.
- **The reward panel and the upgrade panel are handsome and legible.** Cards on dark plaques, icons, prices. The decision to keep the 2D panels was right.
- **The architecture is clean.** Every fix below is local. Nothing here needs a redesign.

### What is broken, worst first

| # | Problem | Why it is the priority it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **You have no idea where to go.** A 24×24 grid, three rooms, no map, no compass, no marker, no objective text. You wander a maze of near-identical corridors hoping to bump into a blue glow. | This is the single biggest reason it "feels weird". Everything else is polish; this is the game not telling you what it wants. |
| 2 | **The cards cover the fight.** At the 2× HUD scale a card is 164×318 px on a 720p screen. Five of them hide the enemies completely — in the fight shot the two creatures are entirely behind the hand, only their name tags peeking out. | The fight is the game, and you cannot see it. |
| 3 | **Enemy tags collide.** Two enemies at similar depth project to nearly the same screen point; the tags stack and neither is readable. The HP bar reads as a floating red stub. | You cannot see what you are fighting or what it is about to do. |
| 4 | **"End turn" is clipped off the right edge.** Positioned at `ui.size.x - 70`, and the button auto-sizes wider than 70. | The only button in the fight is half off-screen. |
| 5 | **No menus at all.** No main menu, no pause, no options, no way to quit without Alt+F4, no volume, no mouse sensitivity. | Requested, and a game you cannot quit from is not a build you can hand anyone. |
| 6 | **Nothing dims behind a panel.** Bone-white text over bright ochre stone. | Panels are legible in spite of the background, not because of it. |
| 7 | **No crosshair.** A first-person game where you click enemies to target them. | You are aiming at nothing. |
| 8 | **No Soul counter.** The upgrade panel prices things in Soul and never says how much you have. | You cannot make the decision the panel exists for. |
| 9 | **The floor banner is off-centre, outlives its welcome and overlaps panels.** Visible in three of the five shots, including on top of the reward panel. | |
| 10 | **Pixel-art candle props over photoreal stone.** 2D decoration inherited from the pixel-art build, floating on the dungeon floor in the reward panel. | The one thing in the game that reads as a mistake rather than a placeholder. |
| 11 | **The guild gives you nothing.** You spawn in a dark empty room facing away from everything, with no HUD, no hint and no title. | First thirty seconds of the game. |
| 12 | **The walking view is one flat orange.** Torch pools blow out to white and everything else is the same amber. | The corridor shot has contrast because it looks *along* a corridor; standing in a room it is flat. |

### What is not addressed here, and why

- **Enemies and ghosts are still placeholders.** Unchanged; it needs a model, not code.
- **Head bob, footsteps, positional audio.** Real gaps, but they are feel work that wants a person playing between each change, and this batch is about the things that are wrong on inspection.

## Batches

### Batch A — the fight is readable

**Files:** `game/hud/hand_view.gd`, `game/hud/enemy_tag.gd`, `game/fight/fight_director.gd`, `game/hud/crosshair.gd` (new), tests for each.

- Cards render at `HandView.CARD_SCALE` (0.62), so a five-card hand occupies the bottom strip instead of the screen. The fan is recomputed in scaled space.
- Hovering a card raises and enlarges it — you read a card by pointing at it, not by having all five permanently legible.
- `EnemyTag` positions are de-collided: tags that land within `STACK_GAP` of one another are pushed apart vertically, in a pure function that a test can check.
- The end-turn button is anchored to the bottom-right corner rather than positioned by arithmetic, so it cannot be clipped.
- A crosshair: a dot that grows into a ring when something targetable is under it.

### Batch B — you know where to go

**Files:** `game/hud/compass.gd` (new), `game/hud/prompts.gd`, `game/world/crawl.gd`, tests.

- A compass strip across the top with a marker per unresolved encounter and one for the stairs, placed by bearing relative to where you are looking. Pure bearing maths, tested.
- Markers dim with distance so a room across the floor reads as further than one through the wall.
- The objective line in `Prompts` says how many rooms are left, or that the stairs are open.

### Batch C — panels sit on something

**Files:** `game/hud/hud_root.gd`, `game/hud/wallet_strip.gd` (new), `game/world/crawl.gd`, `game/hud/choice_screen.gd`, `game/hud/epitaph_screen.gd`, `game/hud/offline_summary.gd`, `game/hud/seance_screen.gd`.

- `HudRoot` draws a dim+vignette behind whatever panel is open.
- A Soul/rate strip at the top of every guild panel.
- An "Esc" hint on panels that can be closed.
- The pixel candle/skull props come out of the panels that now sit over 3D.

### Batch D — menus

**Files:** `game/hud/menu.gd` (new), `game/hud/options_menu.gd` (new), `core/settings.gd`… **no** — settings are presentation, so `game/settings.gd` (new), `game/world/crawl.gd`, tests.

- **Pause menu** on Escape when no panel is open: Resume · Options · Abandon run (only mid-run) · Quit to desktop.
- **Options**: mouse sensitivity, invert Y, FOV, master volume, fullscreen. Applied live.
- Settings persist to `user://settings.cfg`, load on boot, and are pure enough to test.
- Escape's meaning becomes one rule: close the topmost thing; if nothing is open, pause.

### Batch E — the guild reads

**Files:** `game/world/guild_room.gd`, `game/theme/grade.gd`, `game/world/crawl.gd`.

- Spawn facing the well instead of away from it.
- Each station gets a lit sign so you can see there is something there from across the room.
- A little more contrast in the walking grade.

### The look pass

The single biggest change, and it was a colour problem, not a lighting problem:

- **Ambient fill 0.085 → 0.22, cold blue.** With almost no fill, the only light in the game was the torches, so every surface was a value of the same orange and the image had no colour contrast anywhere. That is what "muy sombrío" was describing.
- **Stone tinted cool-grey** (`Kit.STONE_TINT`). The CC0 sets are warm sandstone; tinting the albedo pulls the colour out of the texture so it comes from the lighting instead. A stone wall is grey and looks orange because it is lit by fire.
- **Torches 5.0 → 3.6, tonemap white 4.0 → 8.0.** Every torch was clipping to pure white, and a blown highlight has no colour — the brightest thing in the frame was also the least warm.
- **Hero torch 2.6 → 4.4.** With the fill raised, a weak hero torch left everything within arm's reach blue, which is the one place the player's own light should win.
- Added SSIL, stronger glow with an HDR threshold, and one `adjustment` block (saturation 1.18, contrast 1.06).

### Bugs the screenshots found that no test could

1. `FOG_MODE_DEPTH` ignores `fog_density` entirely — switching to it silently turned the fog off over the twenty metres a corridor spans.
2. `_face()` aimed the camera at the room's geometric centre, so walking in from a corner left the enemies off to one side of the screen.
3. Ghost figures blew out to pure white under the new headroom, **and** stood on room centres — the same point the fight stages on, so a ghost ended up inside the enemy.
4. The vitals had no backing plate: bar, orbs and numbers floated loose on lit stone, each legible, the group reading as debris.

### Deliberately not done, with reasons

- **No "Abandon run" in the pause menu.** `core/` has no abandon action — the only way out of a run is the exit decision at phase `exit`. Whether abandoning kills the hero, banks the Soul, or counts as a death for the ladder is an **economy** question, and a pause menu does not get to decide it. Quitting saves mid-run and resumes, so nothing is trapped.
- **Batch B (compass / where to go) and batch E (guild reads) are still open.** They are the #1 item in the review and still the biggest gameplay problem.

### The one core change, and why it is safe

`abandon` is the only line added to `core/` in this whole stage. It resolves as
a **retreat**, which the economy already prices: the hero lives, you keep what
you banked, you earn nothing new. That makes abandoning strictly worse than
playing on unless you are about to die — which is what a retreat is for — so it
introduces no new dominant strategy.

It is deliberately **not** in `legal_actions`. If it were, the fight autopilot
would be offered it, the balance simulator would start abandoning runs, and
every §12 invariant would silently begin measuring a different game. Verified:
all three demos are still byte-identical to `main`.

### Bugs found by running it, not by reading it

- A prop with a collider spawned beside the guild's upgrade table and **walled
  the player out of it**. Caught by `crawl_walkthrough_test`, which drives the
  real scene with real key presses. Fixed with a keep-clear ring around every
  cell the player must be able to reach.
- Leaving the guild while standing in a station crashed: `Area3D` emits
  `body_exited` on the frame *after* the guild was freed. It happened on every
  single descent.

### Still open

- **Rigged enemy models.** Unchanged and unchangeable from here — it needs art.
  The silhouettes buy time; they are not a substitute.
- **Cards as real 3D objects** in a screen-space overlay (the Hearthstone
  approach). Recommended, scoped, not built: half-built it would be worse than
  the current flat cards.
- **Head bob, footsteps, positional audio.** Feel work that wants a person
  playing between each change.
