# M2 Stage 12 — Tending past restless

Spec §5.5. The first Soul sink that is an investment rather than a repair.

## What shipped as a stub

M1's Tend cleared restless and nothing else, which the file said out loud: "a
minimal Tend that clears restless". The spec asks for two more: "spend Soul to
**upgrade a card** in a ghost's deck or **add a relic from the compendium**;
the ghost re-simulates (its measured stats are kept as the baseline and the
simulated delta is applied)."

That last parenthesis needed no special case. `CampaignEngine.strength_for`
already blends a ghost's measured record with a fresh simulation — it is how
pricing a ghost has always worked — so tending is: change the deck, re-price,
and the spec's sentence falls out.

## The compendium

Spec §5.5 names it and §6.1 lists it among the things a prestige keeps, and it
did not exist. It does now: every relic the guild has ever held, recorded when
a run ends, kept through the rite.

The point of arming *from the compendium* rather than from the whole roster is
that it makes a relic matter after the run it was found on. You cannot give a
ghost something the guild has never seen.

## Chosen, not picked

The card a Sharpen upgrades is the most valuable one the ghost still carries
un-upgraded, by the same `CardValue` the auto-draft uses. The relic an Arm
hands over is the first in the compendium the ghost is not already carrying.

This is a design decision and not only a scope one. The interesting question
in tending is *which ghost, and which of the three tends* — a floor-20 Arm
costs seven times a floor-3 Sharpen, and the guild has one purse. Which of
eleven near-identical Strikes gets the plus is not a decision, and a picker
over a dead hero's deck would be a modal inside a modal.

## The bug this nearly shipped with

`fixed_strength` means "priced once and never again": the Founder is a flat
number out of `balance.json`, and an expedition ghost was priced the hour it
launched. Sharpening either of them would have taken the Soul, upgraded the
card and left the strength exactly where it was — the worst kind of bug,
because the player is told it worked. Tending clears the flag: a ghost that
has been re-made is priced by what it is now.

## Echoes

Spec §5.5: "echoes stay linked to their source: tending the source updates all
its echoes." `_reprice` copies the source's deck and relics down to every echo
of it and re-prices them. Without it, an echo would be the one place in the
economy where a copy silently stops being a copy.

## Self-review

Costs are the same curve as the restless tend with a multiplier, so all three
scale with the ghost's floor together and there is one number to tune rather
than three. Arm is dearest because a relic fires in every fight the ghost will
ever have.

All four demos are byte-identical: nothing here happens without the player
spending Soul on it.
